Club volunteers · Field test kit

Play a real round. Send back the numbers.

Before the club says the scorer keeps up with UDisc Pro, it needs measurements from real phones on a real course. This sheet is the whole job: what to write down, the round script with its interruptions, and how to send the log.

  • Two phones one iPhone, one Android
  • Two rounds 18 holes for four, then doubles
  • Private no other player's name leaves the phone

1 Before you tee off

Write these down for each phone. The field log records the app build, cache version, screen and network type on its own.

Keep screen brightness fixed (auto-brightness off) and Low Power Mode off. Turn Wi-Fi off on the course. Comparing with UDisc? Note its version and plan, do the same tasks, and switch which app goes first each time.

2 Turn on Field test mode

  1. Sign in at gvdgclub.com. If you installed the app, open it from the Home Screen icon.
  2. Open Account, then under Devices turn on Field test mode. In the scorer it is in Tools.
  3. Open Account → Field log and type a phone name, such as “Pixel 7 · Chrome”.
  4. Start or join the round. A small FIELD TEST pill shows on the scorecard. Tap it to add notes.
  5. iPhone only: tap the pill, enter Battery % at the start and the end. Android records battery every 5 minutes.

The log stays on your phone until you export it. It records timings, connection changes, your own member id and round codes. It never records other players' names or PDGA numbers.

3 Round 1: 18 holes, four players

Both phones join the same card by code and both enter every player's score. Tap the matching note chip just before each interruption. If a friend can keep the round's watch link open on another phone with Field test mode on, it measures spectator delay.

48-hour interruption (separate day): stop a card after hole 9, leave it 48 hours so the sign-in expires, then reopen, sign in and finish. Note whether every saved score synced exactly once. Low storage: if a phone has under 1 GB free, tap the Low storage chip at the start.

5 What to note by hand

Watch steps, when the watch builds are available

The native watch apps are not distributed yet, so skip this for now. Later: pair the watch from Round tools, score holes 3–5 from the watch, make a phone and watch edit at the same time on hole 9, and note watch battery at the start and end.

6 Send the log

  1. Open Account → Field log. Check the summary: score feedback, server save, offline time, rounds finished.
  2. Tap Export and share both files, the JSON and the CSV, with the club organizer by email or text. If sharing does not open, use Download files and attach them.
  3. Send a photo of this sheet with your notes.
  4. Keep the log until the organizer confirms they have it. Then use Clear log and turn Field test mode off.

Results are reported per phone, with medians and ranges. One good round on one phone is not a reliability claim, and any lost score, privacy or accessibility problem blocks a release.
