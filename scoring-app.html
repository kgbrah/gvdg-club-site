The details behind the score

Give every round
something to tell you.

Your total is the starting point. GVDG shows how you got there, which parts of your game hold up, and where you sit among the players you know.

01 / Track the shot

See what led to the score.

Mark your lies to build fairway, C1/C2 in regulation, parked, putting, and scramble statistics. Review saved marked lies in a round replay.

Shot stats use recorded lies and mapped tee/basket positions. They are estimates, not automatic shot detection.

02 / Separate your rounds

Give each format its own context.

Keep casual and competitive stats separate. Filter by singles or doubles, then stroke play or match play. See a separate scoring mix for available PDGA rounds.

PDGA scorecards supply scoring results, not missing shot-level stats.

03 / Find your place

Put your numbers next to the club.

See your percentage, makes or hits out of attempts, and club rank for each tracked category. Sample minimums help keep a handful of shots from topping the standings.

Member season views connect recorded results to your club account.

Your history belongs here, too

Bring your UDisc rounds with you.

Import a UDisc CSV to explore your scoring average, best round, bogey-free rounds, and hardest/easiest holes. Imported history stays separate from club ranks and handicap. The import brings in hole scores, not detailed putting or fairway data.

How do the stat definitions compare?

GVDG estimates fairways from marked positions relative to the tee-to-basket line. C1 putting includes short putts; C1X excludes tap-in distances of 3.3 meters or less. Unknown and incomplete samples are labeled. Parked means within 10 feet in GVDG and about 11 feet in UDisc. GVDG scrambling measures par-or-better after missing C1 in regulation; UDisc uses holes with an off-fairway or OB throw. These percentages should not be treated as interchangeable. A little tracking. A clearer picture.

From your first throw
to your shot stats.

Use a layout with mapped tees and baskets. Mark the landing spots as you play so your scorecard has the detail behind each hole.

  1. 01

    Throw. Walk. Mark.

    After your tee shot, stand at your disc and tap Mark lie. If the first tap requests location access, wait for your position and tap again.

    Start with where your tee shot landed. You do not need to mark the tee.

  2. 02

    Follow the disc.

    Mark each next landing spot before you throw again, including missed putts. The button advances to Lie 2, Lie 3, and so on.

    An accidental mark? Use Undo to remove the most recent one.

  3. 03

    Hole out. Enter the score.

    When the disc is in the basket, enter your final hole score. The score accounts for the holed-out throw; you do not need to mark the basket as another lie.

    Keep your scores and marked lies accurate throughout the card.

  4. 04

    Finish the card.

    Once all scores agree, choose Finish card. Review Confirm scores, then tap Looks good — lock card to finish.

    Sign in as a member before playing to connect your rounds to your club account.

You can also tap a landing spot on the hole map with Measure mode off. Scores and marked lies save on this device when the connection drops. Check the sync status before finishing your card. Location accuracy, incomplete recording, and mapped positions affect the estimates.

Put the club on your Home Screen.

Keep GVDG alongside your other apps. You can also keep using it directly in your browser.

iPhone / Safari

Add GVDG to your Home Screen.

  1. Open gvdgclub.com in Safari.
  2. Open Share and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

If the option is missing, scroll to Edit Actions in the Share menu. Apple's instructions ↗

Android / Chrome

Install from your browser.

  1. Open gvdgclub.com in Chrome.
  2. Open the three-dot menu and choose Install and create shortcut, then Install.
  3. Follow the confirmation prompts, then open GVDG Club from your phone.

Some Chrome versions call this Install app or Add to Home screen. Google's instructions ↗

The installed app opens the member dashboard. Choose Keep score in the menu to start a casual round. On a phone, open the menu button first.

Do I need to join the club to keep score?

No club login is required to start, join, or watch a casual round. The member dashboard and club features use your member account. Explore membership.

How do I get the detailed shot stats?

Use Mark lie during your round on a mapped layout. Fairway, circle, and putting stats depend on those recorded positions. Entering only final hole scores builds your scoring mix, not a complete shot history. Follow the Mark lie walkthrough.

Can I keep using UDisc too?

Yes. GVDG's Add to UDisc helper opens the course in UDisc and shows your scores to copy. It is manual re-entry, not automatic account syncing.

Can I add GVDG to my Home Screen?

Yes. Add the club app from Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, then open it from the GVDG Club icon. See the phone installation steps.
