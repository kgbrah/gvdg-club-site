The details behind the score

Give every round
something to tell you.

Your total is the starting point. GVDG shows how you got there, which parts of your game hold up, and where you sit among the players you know.

01 / Track the shot

See what led to the score.

Record throws, putts, penalties, and outcomes on the holes you want to track. Saved shots can support fairway, regulation, parked, putting and scramble statistics.

The app labels incomplete records and estimates based on older marked positions.

02 / Separate your rounds

Give each format its own context.

Keep casual and competitive stats separate. Filter by singles or doubles, then stroke play or match play. See a separate scoring mix for available PDGA rounds.

PDGA scorecards supply scoring results, not missing shot-level stats.

03 / Find your place

Put your numbers next to the club.

See your percentage, makes or hits out of attempts, and club rank for each tracked category. Sample minimums help keep a handful of shots from topping the standings.

Member season views connect recorded results to your club account.

Import a UDisc CSV to explore your scoring average, best round, bogey-free rounds, and hardest/easiest holes. Imported history stays separate from club ranks and handicap. The import brings in hole scores, not detailed putting or fairway data.

The rest of the app

Score, rank, and stay with the club.

The same account that holds your theme also holds your rounds, your bag tag, and the people you play with.

  • Open a link and play.

    Start or join a casual card by code in your phone's browser. No club login is required to keep score. Sign in when you want the round on your club account.

  • The format you are playing.

    Score stroke play or match play. Filter club stats by singles or doubles, and keep casual rounds separate from competitive ones.

  • Ratings you can tell apart.

    Your PDGA official rating on Home and Stats, a club rating from the rounds you play here, plus a playing estimate during a round on supported layouts. A club estimate is not an official PDGA rating.

  • League night, in the app.

    Bag tags and challenges, event registration, the member wallet, club standings, and direct messages between members.

  • When the signal drops.

    Prepare a course pack, then score an offline round on a saved layout. Scores already on an open card can wait until you reconnect. Joining a live card still needs a connection.

  • A card you can read on your wrist.

    Scoring includes a large-target wrist layout, distance alerts while the screen stays awake, and local traffic from cards that are sharing. Native watch apps are in device testing and are not in the stores yet.

A little tracking. A clearer picture.

From your first throw
to your shot stats.

Use a mapped layout to save positions. Add a throw, putt, or penalty to record what happened on each stroke.

  1. 01

    Throw. Walk. Mark.

    After your tee shot, stand at your disc and tap Mark lie. If the first tap requests location access, wait for your position and tap again.

    Start with where your tee shot landed. You do not need to mark the tee.

  2. 02

    Follow the disc.

    In the shot record, choose Add throw, Add putt, or Add penalty. Record the lie and outcome you observed.

    Use Undo to remove an accidental position mark.

  3. 03

    Hole out. Enter the score.

    Record a made outcome for the finishing throw or putt. Enter the full hole score, including any penalties.

    Keep your scores and marked lies accurate throughout the card.

  4. 04

    Finish the card.

    Once all scores agree, choose Finish card. Review Confirm scores, then tap Looks good — lock card to finish.

    Sign in as a member before playing to connect your rounds to your club account.

You can also tap a landing spot on the hole map with Measure mode off. Scores and marked lies save on this device when the connection drops. Check the sync status before finishing your card. Location accuracy, incomplete recording, and mapped positions affect the estimates.

Do I need to join the club to keep score?

Anyone can start or join a casual card. A valid shared watch link opens the spectator view in a browser. The member dashboard uses your club account. Explore membership.

How do I get the detailed shot stats?

Use the shot record to log throws, putts, penalties and outcomes. Map positions add distance and location context. Final hole scores alone still build your scoring mix. Follow the Mark lie walkthrough.

Can I keep using UDisc too?

Yes. GVDG's Add to UDisc helper opens the course in UDisc and shows your scores to copy. It is manual re-entry, not automatic account syncing.

Lost a disc, or found one?

Post it in Lost & Found on the site, or from Round tools in the scoring app. No club login needed: anyone can post after a quick email check. The club matches found and lost discs, reads any writing on a found disc privately, and lets the likely owner know. See how it works.

Can I sign up for the club's PDGA events here?

Yes. When GVDG runs a PDGA-sanctioned event, register on its event page here, with no Disc Golf Scene fee. Other PDGA events register on their own page (usually Disc Golf Scene), and Register in the app takes you there. See PDGA in the app.

Can I add GVDG to my Home Screen?

Yes. Add the club app from Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, then open it from the GVDG Club icon. See the phone installation steps.
